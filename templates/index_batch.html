Quadratic Acceleration Testing

Experimental sandbox isolated from Batch Generation and benchmarks. Explores fobs(t) = fsrc(t) × Doppler(t) where emitted frequency couples to speed/RPM under kinematic acceleration — RPM-dependent pitch, acceleration-aware source evolution, combined engine-pitch + Doppler, and nonlinear/quadratic frequency change. No CV mode; acceleration-focused only. Outputs use a single flat folder per sample (no Common/Essential copies): WAV, .npy features, diagnostic PNGs, aligned overlay, plus three spectrogram views (CQT, wide-band STFT, narrow-band STFT).

Source Frequency Model (Pre-Doppler)

g(v/vref) scales the source spectrum before geometric Doppler warping. Compare kinematic-only vs RPM-linear vs RPM-quadratic coupling.
